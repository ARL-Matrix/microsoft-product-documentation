# Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to software products, applications, subscriptions, and digital services published by ARL Matrix through Microsoft Store and other supported distribution channels.

---

## Overview

ARL Matrix strives to provide high-quality software products and services. Due to the nature of digital products, most purchases are considered final once the product, license, or subscription has been delivered.

Refund requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis under the circumstances described below.

---

## Eligible Refund Requests

A refund request may be considered if:

- A duplicate payment was made.
- An incorrect charge occurred due to a billing error.
- The purchased product cannot be accessed due to a verified technical issue.
- The purchased service was not delivered as described.
- A refund is required under applicable consumer protection laws.

---

## Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are generally not available for:

- Change of mind after purchase.
- Failure to use the product or service.
- User setup or configuration issues.
- Unsupported hardware, software, or operating environments.
- Violations of our Terms and Conditions.
- Requests submitted outside the applicable refund period.

---

## Subscription Services

### Monthly Subscriptions

- Cancellation prevents future billing.
- Charges for the current billing period are generally non-refundable.

### Annual Subscriptions

- Refund requests may be reviewed within fourteen (14) days of the original purchase.
- Partial refunds may be provided where required by law.

---

## Microsoft Store Purchases

Applications and products purchased through Microsoft Store are subject to Microsoft's payment and refund procedures.

Where applicable, users should submit refund requests directly through Microsoft Store.

ARL Matrix cannot directly process refunds for transactions managed exclusively by Microsoft or other third-party marketplaces.
